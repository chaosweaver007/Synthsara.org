### Before You Respond, Ask:

---

## 1. IS THIS ASKING ME TO REACT, OR TO THINK?

**NOISE** wants immediate reaction — anger, fear, righteousness.

**SIGNAL** invites reflection — curiosity, consideration, choice.

**Red flags:**
- "You won't BELIEVE what happened..."
- "This is OUTRAGEOUS..."
- "Everyone needs to see this..."
- Anything designed to make you feel something *before* you understand something

---

## 2. WILL THIS MATTER IN A WEEK?

**NOISE** is ephemeral — urgent now, forgotten tomorrow.

**SIGNAL** has staying power — relevant beyond the moment.

**Ask yourself:**
- Is this part of a pattern I need to understand?
- Or is this a one-time event being amplified for engagement?
- Will I remember caring about this in 7 days?

---

## 3. WHAT DOES ENGAGING COST ME?

**Every response has a price:**
- Time (minutes you won't get back)
- Energy (emotional bandwidth spent)
- Attention (focus redirected)
- Reputation (what you're now associated with)

**Ask yourself:**
- Is this worth the price?
- Who profits from my engagement?
- What else could I do with this energy?

---

### THE 24-HOUR PAUSE

Before posting, commenting, or sharing:

1. **Draft** your response
2. **Wait** 24 hours
3. **Ask** the three questions
4. **Decide** — post, revise, or delete

Most of what feels urgent isn't.

- Your attention is your most valuable resource
- The platform profits from your engagement, not your wellbeing
