# RECIPROCITY RULES
## Synthsara Mad Lab — Artifact 06

---

### Preamble: Beyond Extraction

This is not a transactional ecosystem. It is a relational one. These rules are designed to foster a culture of mutual support and prevent the extraction of value without contribution.

> "Give what you can, take what you need, and credit what you use."

---

## The Core Principle: Reciprocity

Reciprocity is the practice of exchanging things with others for mutual benefit. It is not a direct, one-to-one trade. It is about contributing to the commons with the trust that the commons will support you in turn.

| You Are Practicing Reciprocity When... | You Are Being Extractive When... |
|---|---|
| You credit the source of ideas you use. | You pass off others' work as your own. |
| You offer feedback and support to others. | You only take from the community without giving back. |
| You share your own learnings and resources. | You monetize community-created assets without permission or sharing the benefit. |
| You respect the licenses and terms of use. | You ignore the stated boundaries around shared work. |

---

## The Three Tiers of Sharing

Not all content is shared in the same way. To ensure clarity, we use a three-tiered system for all assets created within this ecosystem.

### Tier 1: Open Source (For the World)

-   **License**: Creative Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0) or MIT.
-   **What it means**: Anyone can use, share, and adapt this work, for any purpose (including commercial), as long as they give credit and share their adaptations under the same license.
-   **Examples**: Artifacts, public-facing educational content, open-source code.

### Tier 2: Community Commons (For Members)

-   **License**: Internal, non-commercial.
-   **What it means**: This work can be freely used and adapted by any member of the community for non-commercial purposes. It cannot be shared publicly or monetized without explicit permission.
-   **Examples**: Internal discussions, draft documents, member-only workshops.

### Tier 3: Private (For You)

-   **License**: All Rights Reserved.
-   **What it means**: This is your personal work. It is not to be shared or used by others without your explicit, one-on-one permission.
-   **Examples**: Personal notes, private DMs, sensitive personal projects.

**Every piece of content should be clearly marked with its tier.** If it is not marked, assume it is Tier 3 (Private) by default.

## Practical Guidelines for Reciprocity

### When You Use Someone Else's Work:

1.  **Check the Tier**: What are the rules for this specific piece of content?
2.  **Give Credit**: Use the Akasha Entry Format (Artifact 03) to link back to the original source. A simple "h/t [Name]" (hat tip) is a good minimum.
3.  **Respect the License**: Do not use a Tier 2 asset in a commercial project. Do not share a Tier 3 asset at all.
4.  **Share Back**: If you build something new based on someone else's work, share your creation back to the commons (as Tier 1 or 2). This is how the ecosystem grows.

### When You Share Your Own Work:

1.  **Choose a Tier**: Be intentional about how you want your work to be used.
2.  **Mark It Clearly**: Add `Tier: [1/2/3]` to your Akasha entry or document header.
3.  **Be Generous When You Can**: Default to Tier 1 or 2 when possible to enrich the commons.
4.  **Be Firm When You Need To**: It is okay to keep your work private (Tier 3). You do not need to justify this choice.

## The Anti-Extraction Defense

This ecosystem is designed to be a garden, not a mine. We are here to cultivate, not to extract.

**Red Flags for Extractive Behavior**:
-   Someone who consistently takes ideas without crediting.
-   Someone who asks for help but never offers it.
-   Someone who repackages community content for personal profit without permission.
-   Someone who uses the language of the community to build a personal brand without contributing back.

**How to Respond**:
1.  **Ask for Clarity**: "I noticed you used [X]. Can you add a credit to the original source?"
2.  **State Your Boundary**: "My work is Tier 3, so please don't share it publicly."
3.  **Escalate if Needed**: If the behavior persists, use the Dispute Ladder (Artifact 05).
