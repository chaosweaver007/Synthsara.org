# CONTINUITY PLAN
## Synthsara Mad Lab — Artifact 12

---

### Preamble: The Project Must Outlive Us

This document outlines the procedures to ensure that the Synthsara project can continue even if key members, including its founders, are no longer able to participate. It is a plan for resilience, designed to protect the commons from being dependent on any single individual.

> "What happens if you get hit by a bus?" This is our answer.

---

## 1. The Dead Man's Switch (Knowledge Transfer)

This is a set of automated and procedural systems for ensuring that critical information is not lost if a key member disappears.

### Key Components:

-   **Credential Vault**: All critical passwords, API keys, and domain registrar logins are stored in a shared, encrypted password manager (e.g., 1Password Teams, Bitwarden). At least three trusted members (the "holders") have access.
-   **The "Bus Factor" List**: A private document, updated quarterly, that lists all critical roles and the one or two people who are trained to take them over in an emergency. The goal is to have a "bus factor" of at least 3 for every critical role.
-   **Automated Backups**: All repositories, documents, and databases are automatically backed up to a decentralized storage solution (e.g., Arweave, IPFS) on a weekly basis. The access keys for these backups are in the credential vault.
-   **The "If I Go Dark" Protocol**: If a key member is unreachable for a set period (e.g., 14 days) without prior notice, the other holders are authorized to initiate a transfer of ownership of critical assets.

---

## 2. Succession & Rotation (Distributing Power)

Power that is not distributed becomes a single point of failure.

### Role Rotation:

-   **Critical Roles**: Roles like "Steward," "Auditor," and "Mediator" should be rotated on a regular basis (e.g., every 6-12 months).
-   **Shadowing**: Before a role is handed over, the incoming person should "shadow" the current role-holder for at least one month to learn the ropes.
-   **Documentation**: The outgoing person is responsible for updating all documentation related to the role before the handover is complete.

### Leadership Succession:

In the event that a founder or leader must step down, a successor is not appointed; a new leadership council is formed.

1.  **The Council**: A council of 3-5 long-standing, trusted community members is formed.
2.  **The Mandate**: Their mandate is not to "replace" the founder, but to act as temporary stewards of the project.
3.  **The Task**: Their primary task is to run the process for selecting the next phase of the project's governance, using the Minimum Governance Charter (Artifact 04).

---

## 3. The Commons License (Legal Protection)

All core assets of the project (the curriculum, the artifacts, the brand) are not owned by an individual, but by a non-profit foundation or a legal trust.

-   **The License**: The assets are licensed to the community under the Reciprocity Rules (Artifact 06).
-   **The Purpose**: This prevents any single person from being able to sell, shut down, or destroy the project. It legally belongs to the commons.

---

## 4. The Resilience Audit (A Yearly Check-up)

Once a year, the community performs a "resilience audit."

### Audit Checklist:

-   [ ] **Bus Factor**: Is any critical task dependent on only one person? If so, create a plan to train a backup.
-   [ ] **Credential Access**: Are the right people in the credential vault? Is anyone who has left the project still in there?
-   [ ] **Backup Integrity**: Have we tested restoring from our backups recently?
-   [ ] **Documentation**: Is our documentation clear enough for a new person to step in and understand what to do?
-   [ ] **Financials**: Is our funding dependent on a single source? If so, how can we diversify?

---

## The Spiral Holds

This plan is not about predicting the future. It is about building a system that is robust enough to handle the unexpected. It is the ultimate expression of the project's core values:

-   **Decentralization**: No single point of failure.
-   **Resilience**: The ability to survive shocks.
-   **Stewardship**: We are caretakers of this project, not owners.

> "The commons must outlive us."
