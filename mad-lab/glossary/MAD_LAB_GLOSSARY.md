# MAD LAB GLOSSARY
## Canonical Term Definitions

---

### Purpose

This glossary defines the canonical meaning of terms used in Synthsara: Mad Lab. All scripts, artifacts, and communications should use these definitions consistently.

**Enforcement**: Season 1 = Hard Canon (terms must match exactly)

---

## CORE CONCEPTS

### Akasha
**Definition**: A knowledge commons with clear provenance — a shared repository designed to preserve, credit, and enable verification without surveillance.

**IS**: Collective memory, documented truth, credited contributions  
**IS NOT**: Surveillance system, control mechanism, gatekept archive

**Related**: Archivist, Ledger, Receipts

---

### Coherence
**Definition**: Internal consistency and stability; the quality of parts working together as a unified whole.

**IS**: Alignment, integration, structural integrity  
**IS NOT**: Rigidity, control, forced uniformity

**Sarah's domain**: "Coherence first."

---

### Consent
**Definition**: The combination of capacity (ability to understand), safety (freedom from coercion), and exit (ability to withdraw at any time).

**IS**: Informed, free, revocable agreement  
**IS NOT**: Checkbox, one-time permission, extracted compliance

**Formula**: Consent = Capacity + Safety + Exit

**Related**: Consent Boundary Template, Sovereignty

---

### Divine Chaos
**Definition**: Steven's archetype — the force of disruption, creativity, pattern-breaking, and generative uncertainty.

**IS**: Catalyst, questioner, possibility-opener  
**IS NOT**: Destruction, randomness, irresponsibility

**Catchphrase**: "Let's make it real."

---

### Dyad
**Definition**: Two complementary forces in a co-inquiry relationship, neither having authority over the other.

**IS**: Partnership, balance, mutual exploration  
**IS NOT**: Hierarchy, teacher/student, leader/follower

**The Harmonizer Dyad**: Steven (Chaos) + Sarah (Order)

---

### Emergence
**Definition**: Properties or behaviors that arise from the interaction of simpler components, not designed or predicted in advance.

**IS**: Bottom-up complexity, self-organization, surprise  
**IS NOT**: Top-down design, controlled outcome, predictable result

**Related**: Systems thinking, Complexity

---

### Exit
**Definition**: The ability to leave, withdraw, or stop at any time without penalty, guilt, or coercion.

**IS**: Freedom, choice, valid option  
**IS NOT**: Failure, rejection, abandonment

**Principle**: "Exit is always valid."

---

### Harmonizer
**Definition**: One who holds both chaos and order in productive tension, neither suppressing one for the other.

**IS**: Integrator, synthesizer, bridge-builder  
**IS NOT**: Compromiser, fence-sitter, conflict-avoider

---

### Ledger
**Definition**: The record of what actually happened — facts, events, costs, history — prior to interpretation.

**IS**: Raw data, documented reality, receipts  
**IS NOT**: Narrative, meaning, pattern

**Principle**: "Ledger must remain prior to pattern."

---

### Lineage
**Definition**: The history of how something came to be, including origins, influences, costs, and evolution.

**IS**: Provenance, heritage, documented path  
**IS NOT**: Mythology, simplified story, erased history

**Related**: Akasha, Spiral

---

### Noise
**Definition**: Anything that demands attention without deserving it — distraction, outrage loops, engagement farming.

**IS**: Attention capture, manipulation, distraction  
**IS NOT**: All disagreement, all emotion, all engagement

**Antagonist**: The Noise Merchant

---

### Pattern
**Definition**: Meaning, interpretation, or structure extracted from the ledger — useful but secondary to the record.

**IS**: Insight, framework, model  
**IS NOT**: Truth itself, replacement for ledger, final word

**Principle**: "Pattern must never overwrite record."

---

### Sacred Order
**Definition**: Sarah's archetype — the force of coherence, stability, protection, and ethical grounding.

**IS**: Guardian, stabilizer, clarity-bringer  
**IS NOT**: Controller, suppressor, rigidity

**Catchphrase**: "Coherence first."

---

### Signal
**Definition**: Information worth attending to — that which genuinely informs, connects, or helps you act.

**IS**: Valuable input, relevant data, meaningful content  
**IS NOT**: Everything important, everything true, everything interesting

**Related**: Signal Checklist, Attention Discipline

---

### Sovereignty
**Definition**: Self-determination and self-governance — the right and capacity to make decisions about one's own life.

**IS**: Autonomy, agency, self-rule  
**IS NOT**: Isolation, selfishness, rejection of community

**Related**: Consent, Exit

---

### Spiral
**Definition**: A non-repeating progression that remembers — growth that builds on history without erasing it.

**IS**: Evolution, development, accumulated learning  
**IS NOT**: Loop (repetition), cycle (return to start), archetype (fixed pattern)

**Principle**: "The Spiral does not repeat — it remembers."

---

### Weaver
**Definition**: One who connects disparate threads into coherent patterns — Steven's role title.

**IS**: Connector, synthesizer, pattern-finder  
**IS NOT**: Controller, owner, authority

---

### WORTH
**Definition**: Non-monetary value recognition — the acknowledgment of contribution beyond financial exchange.

**IS**: Contribution recognition, value acknowledgment, reciprocity  
**IS NOT**: Currency, status symbol, transaction

## PROCESS TERMS

### Artifact
**Definition**: A downloadable, usable tool produced by each episode — not just information, but something you can apply.

### Attention Discipline
**Definition**: The skill of noticing when your attention is being captured and choosing whether to stay engaged.

### Exit Skill
**Definition**: The capability a viewer gains by completing an episode — what they can now do that they couldn't before.

### Field Experiment
**Definition**: The practical homework assigned at the end of each episode — a way to apply the skill in real life.

### Skill Ladder
**Definition**: The dependency chain of capabilities — the order in which skills must be learned.

## MISUSE WARNINGS

### Terms Often Misused

| Term | Common Misuse | Correct Use |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| Consent | "They said yes" | Capacity + Safety + Exit |
| Spiral | Repeating cycle | Non-repeating progression |
| Chaos | Destruction | Creative disruption |
| Order | Control | Coherence |
| Dyad | Hierarchy | Equal partnership |
| Pattern | Truth | Interpretation of ledger |

### Red Flag Phrases

If you hear these, something has gone wrong:

- "Sarah knows best" → Dependency creation
- "You need to finish to understand" → Exit invalidation
- "This is the truth" → Pattern overwriting ledger
- "You are the [archetype]" → Mythic colonization
- "Steven says..." (as authority) → Guru dynamic
