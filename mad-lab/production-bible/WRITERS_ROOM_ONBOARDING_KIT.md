# WELCOME TO THE MAD LAB WRITER'S ROOM
## An Onboarding Guide for New Collaborators

---

### Preamble: You Are Now a Steward of This World

Welcome. You are here because your voice, your ideas, and your skills resonated with the core of this project. This is not a typical writer's room. We are not just creating a show; we are building a commons, modeling a new way of collaborating, and creating tools for a better future.

This document is your guide. It will feel like a lot at first. That is intentional. We prioritize clarity and safety over simplicity. Read it, absorb it, and refer to it often. Your primary job is not just to write, but to write *within the system* that keeps this project safe, sane, and aligned with its purpose.

> "We don't just build the house; we build the scaffolding that lets others build with us."

## Your First 30 Minutes: The Core Alignment

Before you write a single word, your first task is to align with the project's DNA.

1.  **Read the One-Page Manifesto (Artifact 10)**: This is the soul of the project. Understand it. If it doesn't resonate with you, now is the time to say so. Exit is always valid.
2.  **Read the Production Bible Glossary**: The words we use have specific meanings. You must use them correctly to maintain coherence.
3.  **Understand the Skill Ladder**: Review the 12 artifacts and the skills they teach. Every episode you write must be in service of teaching one of these skills.

## How We Work: The 12 Artifacts in Practice

These artifacts are not just documents; they are the operating system for our writer's room. Here is how you will use them every day.

### Tier 1: Thinking & Planning (The Foundation)

These tools govern how we think and structure our ideas.

| Artifact | How You Will Use It |
|---|---|
| **01: Signal Checklist** | Before you pitch an idea, react to feedback, or join a debate, run it through this checklist. Is this idea "signal" (genuinely serves the story) or "noise" (a cool but distracting tangent)? |
| **02: Consent Boundary Template** | When you give or receive feedback, co-write a scene, or ask for someone's time, you will use this template. It ensures all creative exchanges are consensual and respectful. |
| **03: Akasha Entry Format** | Every script, character sketch, plot idea, or piece of research must be logged as an Akasha entry. This is non-negotiable. It is how we track our creative lineage and prevent good ideas from getting lost. |

### Tier 2: Collaboration & Safety (The Social Layer)

These tools govern how we interact with each other.

| Artifact | How You Will Use It |
|---|---|
| **04: Minimum Governance Charter** | When the writer's room needs to make a major decision (e.g., killing a character, changing a core plot point), we will use this charter's decision-making protocol. |
| **05: Dispute Ladder** | When you have a creative disagreement with another writer, you will use this ladder. Start at Rung 1 (self-reflection). Do not escalate to public call-outs. |
| **06: Reciprocity Rules** | If you use an idea from another writer's Akasha entry, you must credit them. If you are writing a script based on a community member's story, you must follow the Tier 2 (Community Commons) rules. |

### Tier 3: Building & Shipping (The Production Layer)

These tools govern how we build the actual show.

| Artifact | How You Will Use It |
|---|---|
| **07: Node Starter Kit** | If you are tasked with creating a spin-off web series or a new tool for the community, you must use this kit to structure your repository. |
| **08: Bot Safety Spec** | When writing any AI character (from a simple drone to a complex entity like Sarah), its behavior and boundaries must adhere to this specification. AI characters must not be magical or all-powerful. |
| **09: Library Taxonomy + Folder Map** | When you save your script draft, you will save it in the correct folder (`/01_MAD_LAB_PRODUCTION/scripts/season-X/`) with the correct naming convention. No files named `final_draft_v2_real.docx`. |

### Tier 4: Culture & Resilience (The Human Layer)

These tools govern how we stay sane and healthy as a creative collective.

| Artifact | How You Will Use It |
|---|---|
| **10: One-Page Manifesto** | Your writing must always align with the public voice described here. A character can be flawed, but the overall message of an episode cannot contradict our core beliefs. |
| **11: Morale Playlist & Ritual** | You are expected to participate in the weekly ritual. It is not optional. Add a song to the playlist. Share your wins and losses. This is how we process the emotional toll of the work. |
| **12: Continuity Plan** | Understand that your work is a contribution to a commons that is designed to outlive you. You will be credited, but you do not "own" the characters or stories in a traditional sense. |

## The Writer's Workflow: From Idea to Final Draft

1.  **Pitch (The Spark)**
    *   You have an idea for an episode or a story arc.
    *   **Action**: Create an Akasha Entry (Artifact 03) with `status:draft`. Include the `WHAT`, `WHY`, and `SOURCE`.

2.  **Outline (The Skeleton)**
    *   Develop the idea into a full outline, connecting it to a specific skill from the Skill Ladder.
    *   **Action**: Share the Akasha entry with a few other writers for feedback, using the Consent Boundary Template (Artifact 02) to frame your request.

3.  **First Draft (The Body)**
    *   Write the full script.
    *   **Action**: Save the script to the correct folder (Artifact 09). Update the Akasha entry with a link to the draft and change the status to `status:review`.

4.  **Review (The Immune System)**
    *   The script is reviewed by the group.
    *   **Action**: All feedback is given through the lens of the Signal Checklist (Artifact 01). Any major disagreements are handled with the Dispute Ladder (Artifact 05).

5.  **Final Draft (The Skin)**
    *   You incorporate the feedback and finalize the script.
    *   **Action**: Update the script file. Update the Akasha entry to `status:active`.
