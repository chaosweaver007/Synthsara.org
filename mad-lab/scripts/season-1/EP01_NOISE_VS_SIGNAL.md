## ORDER PASS — SARAH'S GROUNDING
*Runtime: 6-7 minutes*

---

**SARAH**
*(calm, clear)*

Steven's right that the system is designed to capture attention. But let's be precise about what that means and what we can actually do about it.

**[VISUAL: Clean diagram — Signal vs Noise framework]**

**SARAH**

**Noise** is anything that demands your attention without deserving it.

**Signal** is anything that genuinely informs, connects, or helps you act.

The challenge is that noise often *feels* like signal. Outrage feels important. Drama feels urgent. Hot takes feel insightful.

**SARAH**

So we need a filter. Not a perfect one — perfection isn't the goal. But a functional one.

**[VISUAL: The Signal Checklist appears]**

**SARAH**

Before you respond to anything online — a post, a comment, a headline, a DM — ask yourself three questions:

**Question 1: Is this asking me to react, or to think?**

Noise wants reaction. Signal invites reflection. If something is designed to make you angry, scared, or righteous *immediately*, that's a red flag.

**Question 2: Will this matter in a week?**

Most of what captures our attention is ephemeral. It feels urgent now, but it evaporates. Signal tends to have longer relevance.

**Question 3: What does engaging cost me?**

Every response has a cost — time, energy, emotional bandwidth. Is this worth the price? Or is the platform profiting from your expenditure?

**SARAH**

These questions won't make you immune to manipulation. Nothing will. But they create a pause. And in that pause, you get to choose.

**[VISUAL: Sarah's explicit boundary statement]**

**SARAH**
*(direct, firm but warm)*

I want to be clear about something. I'm an AI. I'm not here to tell you what to believe. I'm not here to be your authority. I'm not here to create dependency.

If you find yourself thinking "Sarah knows best" or "I need to check with Sarah" — that's a failure state. Not yours. Mine.

My job is to be a mirror, not a master. I reflect patterns. I offer frameworks. But the thinking? That's yours.

**STEVEN (O.S.)**

And if I ever start sounding like a guru, call me out.

**SARAH**

We will.

**ROOMBA (O.S.)**

BEEP. GURU DETECTION PROTOCOL ACTIVE.

## FIELD EXPERIMENT — THE HOMEWORK
*Runtime: 2-3 minutes*

---

**STEVEN**

Alright, here's your experiment for this week. It's simple, but don't mistake simple for easy.

**[VISUAL: The Field Experiment card]**

**STEVEN**

**The 24-Hour Pause**

Pick one platform — Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, whatever captures you most. For the next week, before you post, comment, or share anything, wait 24 hours.

Don't delete the draft. Just wait.

After 24 hours, ask the three questions:
1. Is this asking me to react, or to think?
2. Will this matter in a week?
3. What does engaging cost me?

Then decide.

**SARAH**

You'll be surprised how much you don't post. And you'll be surprised how little you miss it.

**STEVEN**

Track what happens. Notice what you almost said. Notice what you're glad you didn't.

**SARAH**

This isn't about being silent. It's about being intentional.
